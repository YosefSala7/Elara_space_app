# Customer Journey Map: YallaCart Supplier Dashboard

**Project:** B2B SaaS Dashboard (MVP Validation)
**Phase:** Low-Fidelity Wireframe Usability Testing (Round 1)
**Target Persona:** Local FMCG & Hypermarket Suppliers (Egypt)
**Prepared By:** YallaCart UX Research Team

## Overview
This document summarizes the observed behaviors, direct user quotes, and friction points from our recent usability testing sessions. We tested 15 local suppliers (ranging from regional dairy distributors to local FMCG manufacturers) using our low-fidelity wireframes.

---

### Journey Map Breakdown

| Phase | User Actions | Explicit Feedback | Friction Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Awareness** | Receives an invitation email from their YallaCart account manager. Reviews the landing page value proposition. | "I hope this is faster than emailing Excel sheets back and forth."<br><br>"Sounds like a great way to push my goods to more YallaCart branches across Cairo and Alex." | Minor hesitation regarding digital literacy. Some older suppliers worry if their local warehouse team can manage a digital SaaS portal. |
| **2. Sign-Up** | Enters Commercial Registry Number (*Sagel Togary*) and Tax ID (*Bataka Darebeya*). Completes phone verification via SMS OTP. | "The SMS OTP was very fast."<br><br>"Standard form, easy to fill out my company details." | **Low Friction:** Process is familiar and straightforward. Users appreciate the minimal data entry required to get an account created. |
| **3. First Login** | Enters credentials and lands on the main dashboard for the first time. Encounters a screen full of zero-state analytics widgets and a complex left-hand navigation menu. | "Where do I upload my inventory Excel file?"<br><br>"I don't understand these analytics graphs."<br><br>"This looks like an airplane cockpit. I just want to list my Ramadan products." | **CRITICAL FRICTION:** High drop-off rate (approx. 80%). Total cognitive overload. Users are overwhelmed by complex terminology and empty charts. There are no clear "aisle signs" or calls-to-action guiding them on what to do first. |
| **4. First Task** | Tries to add their first product batch manually after giving up on finding a bulk upload or sync option. | "I clicked 'New Item' but it's asking for a global SKU. I only have local barcodes for my cheeses."<br><br>"Is my product live on the system now? Am I supposed to wait for an admin's approval?" | **High Friction:** Terminology mismatch (e.g., "SKU" vs. "Barcode"). Lack of a guided success state leaves users confused, causing them to abandon the task midway. |

---

### Researcher Notes:
While our technical sign-up flow is highly effective, we are losing our suppliers the moment they cross the threshold into the dashboard. The current wireframes prioritize reporting over onboarding, which is actively alienating our users. Immediate product intervention is required for the "First Login" experience.
